# Decisions

Format: ID · date · decision · why · alternatives rejected. Never edit an old entry; supersede it.

### D-001 · 12 Nov · Track 1 Synthetic Eyes
- Why: college PoC allowed only Track 1 or 4. Track 4 needs ~184 GB of video, GPU-heavy models and a hidden scored subset. Track 1 runs in Blender on a laptop.
- Risk accepted: 60% of the score is judged visually.

### D-002 · 12 Nov · Deliver both Track 1 specs
- The 12 Nov evaluation deck (textures, 3 inputs, videos, `.blend`) and the launch spec (`eyes.move(h, v)`, 35 cm target, 40 cm camera, 10-frame sequence, per-frame JSON) differ. Both are built; the launch spec is cheap once the rig exists.

### D-003 · 12 Nov · Head model = Google GNM Head v3
- "GNM" in the organizer deck is Google's Generative aNthropometric Model (github.com/google/GNM), Apache 2.0 for code and weights, so it can sit in a public repo. Includes eyeballs with separate cornea, eye joints, eyelid/brow expression components and an official UDIM UV layout.
- Rejected: FLAME (licence limits, no eyeball detail). Fallback if GNM cannot be driven in Blender by end of 13 Nov: MPFB CC0 head.

### D-004 · 12 Nov · Export GNM once, build the Blender scene from our own script
- GNM's package needs Python 3.13 and TensorFlow; Blender 4.5 ships Python 3.11. Exporting to `assets/gnm_head.npz` keeps the judge's setup to "install Blender, run one command".
- Rejected: community GNM Blender add-on (licence unclear, extra dependency).

### D-005 · 12 Nov · Textures: two routes, renders decide
- GNM ships no skin texture. Route A: CC0 MakeHuman/MPFB skins baked into the GNM UV layout (the floor). Route B: synthetic non-existent identities generated with a depth-conditioned diffusion model and projected to UV. A Route B set ships only if it beats Route A side by side.
- Iris and sclera are generated procedurally; left and right differ.

### D-006 · 12 Nov · Direction convention
- Organizers have not said whose left "Left" means. Default: left **on screen**; `--frame subject` gives the character's left. Videos carry a caption naming the input; metadata records both.

### D-007 · 12 Nov · Close-up framing for showcase videos
- Reference videos (Chris Jones, "Eye Piece" and "Flexpressions") are eye-region close-ups. A close-up camera shows what is scored and hides the missing hair. The 40 cm launch-spec camera is kept for the spec renders.

### D-008 · 12 Nov · Required outputs before brownie features
- By 17 Nov every required output exists end to end at draft quality. Extras (lid bulge, lashes, tear line, head follow) only after that.

### D-009 · 12 Nov · GO on GNM; no export step (amends D-004)
- Spike passed: head builds in Blender, eyes rotate, eyelids close cleanly. The MPFB fallback is dropped.
- GNM's weights are a plain `.npz` and v3.0 has zero pose correctives, so `truegaze/gnm.py` reads the official file directly with NumPy inside Blender. No Python 3.13 environment, no TensorFlow, no `export_gnm.py`.
- Our controls are stored as coefficient vectors in GNM's own expression space (`assets/controls.npz`, a few KB), so they work on any GNM head, which supports the portability score.


### D-010 · 13 Nov · Generated textures first, photo-based skin on top
- Every texture set is generated by `truegaze/textures.py` from GNM's own UV layout and vertex groups: no outside data, no licence risk, distinct left/right iris by construction, reproducible from a seed.
- This is the floor that guarantees the "5–10 UV maps" output. Photo-based skin albedo (Route A transfer or Route B diffusion) replaces `color.1001` per set only where it beats the generated one in side-by-side renders.
- Texture files are plain per-tile images named with GNM's UDIM numbers (`color.1001.jpg`), so they drop onto any GNM head.
- `textures/` stays out of git until the 17 Nov checkpoint to avoid bloating history while the look changes.

### D-011 · 13 Nov · Lashes, brows and tear line are generated mesh strands with their own shape keys
- GNM has no hair. Blender's hair systems are hard to drive from a headless script and do not follow shape keys reliably, so strands are thin mesh tubes built by `tools/make_hair.py` into `assets/hair.npz`.
- For every eyelid control the lashes are rebuilt from the moved lid margin, and the difference is stored as a shape key. Drivers copy the head's key values (no Python in the driver), so the `.blend` plays without script permissions.
- Lash follicles sit 0.9–1.6 mm out from the wet lid edge: GNM's lid has real thickness, and rooting them at the inner edge buried the roots.

### D-012 · 13 Nov · Launch-spec mapping
- `eyes.move(h, v)`: both eyes aim at one point on a plane 35 cm in front of the eye midpoint; h and v in [-1, 1] move it up to 5 cm (the spec's motion bound). h > 0 is the viewer's right, v > 0 is up: with h = sin θ, v = cos θ this starts at the top and runs clockwise as the viewer sees it.
- Eye rotation is reported as XYZ Euler degrees in the world frame relative to straight ahead; pupil centres in pixels of the 1024×1024 render. Units are written into `metadata.json`.
- The direction check (Left/Right/Up/Down/Reset) is measured from projected pupil positions, not from the commanded angles.

### D-013 · 13 Nov · Seams are measured, not assumed
- `tools/seam_audit.py` finds every UV seam edge of the skin and compares texture colour on both sides. GNM's skin has 142 seam edges; the only ones on the front of the face run along the inner lip line.
- The generator fades random detail out near seams and keeps region colours unblurred there, because neither can match across a seam otherwise. Front-of-face step is now 6/255 (was 12). Remaining larger steps are inside the mouth and on the back of the head.

### D-014 · 13 Nov · Lid bulge as four fitted keys per eye
- Measured: at 32° sideways or 24° vertical gaze the cornea cut up to 1.5 mm into 30–150 lid vertices. `tools/fit_bulge.py` pushes that skin out just enough and smooths it into a mound; `motion.bulge()` weights the keys by (angle / limit)², because the cornea only reaches the lid at larger angles.
- Fitted on the open lid only. During a blink with the eye turned the fit is approximate (blinks last 0.3 s). Lashes do not ride the bulge (under 1.5 mm).

### D-015 · 13 Nov · Photo-based skin from MakeHuman's CC0 skins, transferred in 3D (amends D-010)
- Why: the self-audit named skin as the largest gap in the 60-point texture score. MakeHuman's skins are photo-quality, CC0, cover light/dark, male/female, young/middle-aged/old, and have the same detail density as our 4K map (about 0.11 mm per texel at the eyes).
- How: `tools/transfer_skin.py` fits the GNM head to MakeHuman's head (eye centres → rigid → smooth deformation, with eyelid edges, lip line and nose tip pinned) and stores, per GNM vertex, its place in MakeHuman's UV map. Result: median 0.5 mm, 95% under 1.7 mm from the MakeHuman surface. `truegaze/textures.py` then resamples any MakeHuman skin into GNM's layout.
- Still ours on top: wet tissue colour, lash line and brow follicle shading, roughness, pore relief (now also taken from the photo's fine detail), both irises, sclera.
- Where the two heads cannot be matched the generated skin fills in, generated in the photo's own tone so no edge shows.
- No real, identifiable person: MakeHuman skins are composite artist textures released to the public domain.
- Downloads are pinned by SHA-256 in `truegaze/textures.py`; the tuxfamily server is plain HTTP, so the checksum is what protects a fresh clone.

### D-016 · 13 Nov · Eye socket depth, skin drag, eyelid veins, wireframe breakdown (extends D-014)
- Asked for by Akshat after comparing with the reference video. All four are in.
- **Socket depth:** measured GNM's template lids at 4–13 mm off the eyeball (a real lid is 1–3 mm). `tools/fit_bulge.py` now sinks the hollow between lid and brow by up to 2.9 mm and the groove under the lower lid by up to 1.2 mm, never thinner than 1.6 mm over the eyeball. Stored as always-on keys `socket_left/right`, so the stock GNM topology and UVs are untouched.
- **Skin drag:** lids are stuck to the wet eyeball, so skin near the lid edge turns with the eye by 16% of the gaze angle, fading over about 7 mm (up to 1.4 mm of slide). Keys `drag_<in|out|up|down>_<side>`, weight proportional to gaze angle (`motion.soft_tissue`).
- **Thin skin:** faint blue-green veins around the socket and a soft crease above the upper lid, in the skin colour and relief maps; fainter on dark skin, as in life.
- **Lashes and brows follow** socket, bulge and drag keys (same mechanism as the eyelid controls).
- **Breakdown renders:** `--style clay|wire` on `render.py` and `tools/stills.py`. Wire draws GNM's own quad edges through a Wireframe modifier on a second object sharing the mesh data, so it animates with every key.

### D-017 · 13 Nov · Second critique pass against the reference; hero view
- Nine gaps listed and worked through in `docs/reference_eye_piece.md` (lighting and focus, skin glints, fine wrinkles, sclera, wet lid edge, iris spots, facial down, lashes, lid fold).
- New `hero` view (one eye, 66° to its outer side, hard key, shallow focus) for stills and `render.py --view hero`. The front view stays the default because the brief asks to show both eyes moving.
- Reference frames are never committed; comparisons are made locally.
- Known and accepted: no caustic light pooling in the iris, no caruncle geometry, upper lid does not fold into itself.

### D-018 · 13 Nov · Motion numbers checked against sources; constants moved; motion blur
- `tools/evaluate_motion.py` measures the engine the lab way (saccade duration = time above 30 deg/s) and compares with published ranges. Sources were looked up on 13 Nov; two bands that could not be confirmed (peak/mean velocity, fixation wander) are reported as notes and cannot fail the run.
- Found and fixed: (1) the saccade constant "21 ms + 2.2 ms/deg" could not be confirmed; published fits span 20–30 ms + 2–2.7 ms/deg, so it is now 25 + 2.4 (middle of the range). (2) minimum-jerk saccades were too peaked (1.88 × mean velocity); the profile is now a blend with a cosine (1.68). (3) blink opening lengthened to 240 ms so closing is about 50% faster than opening, as measured on people. (4) spontaneous blink rate was 22/min, above the published 11–19; interval widened, now about 17/min.
- Final-quality video uses motion blur (half-frame shutter): a 30 fps camera smears a 40–100 ms saccade, and without it fast eye jumps look like a cut. Cost: about 22 s per frame instead of 15 s.

### D-019 · 13 Nov · Changes taken from the reference author's own notes
- Read the 49 eye-related posts by Chris Jones in the Blender Artists "Human Progress" thread. Table of what he does and what we changed: `docs/reference_eye_piece.md`, third pass.
- Taken: stronger lid drag (30%), non-linear blink through a fitted half-closed shape (`blink_mid`), caruncle, softer blue-grey limbus, iris with thick cords, crypts and relief, shinier lids and a wet pink lid edge with lashes rooted outside it, less SSS, uneven sclera surface, catchlight in the hero view.
- Not taken, on purpose: caustics (he lost months to them), plica semilunaris, tension maps.

### D-020 · 13 Nov · Iris light pooling, plica, expression wrinkles (asked for by Akshat; extends D-019)
- **Light pooling** is computed in the iris shader from the key-light direction, not path-traced: real caustics need path guiding (CPU only, far too slow here). Stated as an approximation wherever it is shown.
- **Plica** is its free edge only, a fine ridge. A flat ribbon was tried and rejected: it stood off the curved eyeball like a panel.
- **Expression wrinkles** use the reference author's final method: a wrinkle height map revealed by shape-key-driven weights, no tension maps. Drivers are plain averages, so the `.blend` needs no script permission.
- Eyeball scattering switched to Christensen-Burley, skin to random-walk (skin), finest skin relief to Voronoi cells, all from the author's notes.
- Render plan: was `docs/RENDER_PLAN.md` (removed 15 Nov once the renders finished; the order lives in `reproduce.sh`).

### D-021 · 14 Nov · Dark-skin sheen and old-skin wrinkles fixed by rule, light sets untouched
- **Problem 1:** sets 03, 06, 08 showed a grey patch under the eye on the lit side. Test renders with specular off proved it was sheen, not colour: the extra shine round the eye that suits light skin is as strong as the whole diffuse colour on dark skin.
- **Fix:** above a darkness threshold (`smoothstep(0.45, 0.65, dark)`; light sets measure ≤ 0.32, dark sets ≥ 0.71) the eye region gets no extra shine and a duller under-eye, fewer fine relief lines, and darker facial down (`info.json` → `fuzz`). Tuned by eye, not from a measurement.
- **Problem 2:** set 07's photo has wrinkles painted as lines; as flat colour they rendered as orange streaks. **Fix:** for the "old" photo, half of that line detail leaves the colour and returns as relief.
- **Why by rule:** both rules are exactly zero for the light sets, so set 01 and the final videos rendered from it are unchanged and need no re-render.

### D-022 · 14 Nov · Eight look and motion changes from an outside review and web research (list: `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`)
- **Lid shadow on the eyeball** is a shader gradient fixed to the resting lid opening, not traced: the lights are too large to cast it. Standard practice (commercial eye shaders ship an "eye occlusion" layer).
- **Eye whites** darkened to a warm grey; limbal ring darker; set 01's blue toned down; iris pooling strength 2.6 → 1.9.
- **Tear film** is now a concave strip in the corner between lid and eyeball (lower deeper than upper). It only reflects: as solid glass it refracted the dark lid interior and drew a black line.
- **Hero catchlight** is a two-by-three window, placed by mirror geometry so it lands on the cornea. Before, it missed the eye entirely.
- **Lash roots:** shading under the lower lashes cut from 0.35 to 0.12; lashes have 10 points instead of 7.
- **Skin:** pores dull the shine more (0.07 → 0.12), coat 0.12 → 0.16. Tuned by eye.
- **Motion:** `brow_lower` (fitted, never used before) follows down-gaze and blinks; lower lid rises slightly in a blink. Amounts are our own; no measured data was found.
- **Rejected from the review:** concave iris (GNM's chamber already measures about 3 mm, like a real eye), head following gaze (data says the head stays still under 20 to 25 degrees), paler fuzz (undoes D-021).
- All five videos and all stills are re-rendered, because every set changed.

## D-023 — Dark-skin under-eye patch: two causes, both removed (15 Nov)
- **Cause 1:** D-021 raised roughness by 0.22 round the eyes on dark sets. That made a matt island with a visible border under glossy cheeks. Removed.
- **Cause 2:** MakeHuman's dark photo skins have grey eye bags painted in. On dark sets only, the low-frequency tone round the eye (between a 12 px and a 300 px blur at 4K) is flattened by 70%; fine detail stays.
- **Scope:** only sets 03, 06 and 08 change (their colour and roughness files). The other five sets are byte-identical, so the set 01 videos stay valid. Re-rendered: stills of 03, 06, 08, `portability_03`, overviews, the set 03 video.
- **Left over:** a broad whitish sheen on the lit cheek of the dark sets. At thumbnail size it still reads as a grey patch, so this is half a fix. Suspects, not yet tested: the pale down hairs, the oil coat.

## D-024 — Look batch from the council audit (15 Nov)
Tested on stills of sets 01, 03, 07. Amounts tuned by eye against the reference frames.
- **Half-lit pupil (audit task 5):** the 80 cm fill light was mirrored in the cornea and its edge cut the pupil in half. The fill no longer shows in reflections.
- **Hero view (1):** key light moved level with the eye and further to the side (it raked the whole face before), fill cut to almost nothing, exposure down half a stop. There is now a real shadow side. Still brighter than the reference: skin fills our frame, theirs is mostly black.
- **Lashes (2):** upper 9.5 to 8 mm, lower 6.5 to 5 mm, lengths spread wider, one lash per slot along the lid instead of pure chance (no bald gaps), bunching halved, lower lashes straighter. 190 upper and 80 lower per eye.
- **Eye white (3):** 110 vessels instead of 60 with more fine ones, thinner, less red, each with a soft pink halo as if under a clear skin. No change to wetness: the shell is already clear glass; the dry look came from the highlight being hidden under the lid in these views.
- **Iris (4):** colours 30% greyer, centre tint closer to the main colour, collarette brightening halved, limbal ring weaker and wider.
- **Fine lines (6):** arcs round the socket are short now; long ones read as fingerprint rings.
- **Under-eye colour (7):** the eye-bag flattening of D-023 applies to light sets at half strength; the pink rings are gone.
- **Dark-skin sheen (12):** tested: not the down hairs. It is the skin's own reflection, which shows far more on dark colour. Reflection and oil coat are halved on dark sets (`deep` in each set's `info.json`). Weaker, not gone.
- **Not done:** lid margin shelf (8), lower lid and eyeball pull-back (9), head sway (10), set 07 streaks (11).
- Every set changed, so all stills and all videos are rendered again.

## D-025 — Second look batch: lid margin, lower lid, eyeball pull-back, head sway, set 07 relief (15 Nov)
Tested on set 01 stills in seven poses (neutral, down, up, half blink, closed, head tilted), stills of sets 03 and 07, and one draft video of the hero view.
- **Lid margin (audit task 8):** measured first: GNM's lid is not a knife edge. It has a flat shelf 2 to 2.8 mm deep (0.9 mm at the thinnest part of the lower lid). It only looked like a knife edge because the shelf was painted and lit as ordinary lid skin and the lashes rooted in the middle of it. No new geometry: `tools/make_hair.py` finds the shelf's front edge, roots the lashes there and saves it as `margin_uv`; the skin texture paints the shelf pinker, smooth and damp.
- **Lower lid (9):** it rose on down-gaze, which is backwards and cut into the iris. It now rides down with the eye (about 1.6 mm at 30 degrees) and up a little on up-gaze. A blink already lifts it to the closed line through the fitted blink key; that was left alone. Amounts are our own; no measured data was looked up.
- **Eyeball pull-back (9):** the eyeball moves 1 mm straight back while the lids are closed.
- **Head sway (10):** yaw up to about 1.7 degrees, pitch 1 (part of it breathing at 15 per minute), roll 0.5, as slow sine waves with random phase per seed. The eyes counter-turn so the gaze stays on its target. The whole rig object turns about the neck joint, so hair, lids and eye maths follow without a rig rework. Amounts are our own. The launch-spec API (`wrapper/eyes.py`) keeps a still head: it shows the commanded pose only.
- **Set 07 (11):** the folds taken from the photo were returned as relief at 1.6 strength and caught the light as shiny streaks; now 0.8. Milder, still visible under the eye in the front view.
- All sets and all motion changed, so all stills and all videos are rendered again.

## D-026 — Third look batch: lid shadow, eye white, pupil, lashes, crown patch, hero light (16 Nov)
From the 16 Nov audit (tasks 13 to 18). Tested on stills of sets 01, 03 and 07 and one portability head, against the reference frames.
- **Lid shadow (13):** a shading term for it already existed and was too weak to see. Darkening under the upper lid goes from 50 to 80 percent and starts 2 mm below the eye centre; corners from 35 to 50 percent. Still a shading term fixed to the resting lid opening, not a cast shadow.
- **Eye white (14):** base colour in the texture from 0.66 to 0.53 and darker toward the lids; on dark sets the shader dims the whole eye by up to 35 percent (`deep` in `info.json`).
- **Pupil and highlight (15):** pupil edge is a soft band (0.88 to 1.10 of the pupil radius, was 0.96 to 1.04). The key light is a disc, so its mirror image in the cornea is round, not a chip with corners. In the hero view the window highlight sits on the upper iris. Pupil depth: not done; it is not visible at these sizes.
- **Lashes (16):** 16 points per lash (was 10); full thickness for two thirds of the length, then a taper; most of the bend near the root so the tip runs straight; lower lashes stray from their slots. Crossing pairs were not added as such: the random lean already crosses neighbours.
- **Crown patch (17):** cause: seams are filled with generated skin in one tone taken from the cheeks, and the scalp in the photo is darker, so the seam from the crown down the back of the skull showed as a pale strip. The fill now takes the photo's colour averaged over the nearby surface, worked out per vertex so it is equal on both sides of a seam. Seam audit still passes (front step at most 5 of 255).
- **Hero view (18):** key moved high on the camera's side (was level, raking), so the brow shades the socket and the cheek and brow catch the light; fill 0.25, window light 1.2 because it is now the only light on the eyeball. Dark sets get up to one stop more exposure in this view, or the eye is lost.
- **Frame cache:** final-quality frame folders now carry a fingerprint of the code, hair and texture set, so frames from an older look cannot be reused by mistake. Old folders under `renders/tmp/final/` are dead weight and can be deleted by hand.
- Every set and the lashes changed, so all stills and all videos are rendered again.
- **Outcome (17 Nov):** all renders done and pushed (`6042fa7`, `779c3ed`). Audit estimate 74 of 100 (was 71.5). New fault from this batch: in the hero view the eye white on the far side of the iris is a flat dark slab. Not fixed.
